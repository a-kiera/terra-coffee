eco Get to Know Us...

Terra is a local coffee shop rooted in a simple idea: great coffee shouldn’t cost the earth. We pour carefully sourced, freshly roasted coffee into cups made from recycled paper, serve our drinks with reusable straws, and keep single-use waste to a minimum. Nothing goes to waste here, either. Our used coffee grounds are free for anyone to take home and use as compost, so your garden can enjoy a second cup. Whether you’re stopping by for a morning espresso or settling in for the afternoon, every sip at Terra is a small step towards a greener community.

eco Our Menus...

Our menus are made with the planet in mind. Every coffee, from a classic espresso to a cold brew, is made with responsibly sourced beans, and our non-coffee drinks, like the chai, matcha and golden milk lattes, use ingredients from ethical suppliers. Our brunch (served 8am to 1pm) is cooked from locally sourced produce, with seasonal dishes like shakshouka and acai bowls alongside hearty favourites such as the Full English. We buy our pastries from a local bakery, and our Cake of the Day is baked in small batches to cut down on food waste. Whatever you order, it's served with minimal packaging and a lot of care.

Don't forget to let us know of any dietary requirements when ordering.
